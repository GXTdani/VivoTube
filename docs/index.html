VivoTube

An unofficial YouTube client for the PlayStation Vita, written from scratch as homebrew.

VivoTube runs on a PS Vita (or the Vita3K emulator) and plays YouTube videos at up to 720p with the console's own video and audio decoders. It talks to YouTube directly from the console, with no server in between, and it's free and open source under the MIT License.

Download the latest release VivoTube on VitaDB

What it does

  • Home with topic feeds, Shorts, new uploads from your subscriptions, and a personal For you feed built on the console from what you watch and like.
  • Watch with chapters, captions, quality from 144p to 720p, playback speeds from 0.25x to 2x, a play queue, and a mini player that keeps playing while you browse.
  • Search with filters, comments with replies, a vertical Shorts player, and channel pages.
  • Library: history, Watch later, liked videos and playlists, kept on the memory card.
  • Text in every major script, including Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi, Thai, Chinese, Japanese and Korean, and emoji in colour.
  • Built-in updates from the project's GitHub releases.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. When you sign in, VivoTube uses the YouTube API Services to show and change your YouTube subscriptions, likes and playlists, and only when you ask it to. Your sign-in is saved on your Vita and goes only between your Vita and Google. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what VivoTube reads, what it stores, and how to remove it. You can revoke VivoTube's access in your Google Account at any time.

To sign in, choose Sign in in VivoTube and enter the code it shows at google.com/device. More details are in the README.
